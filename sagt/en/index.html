Sagt for Mac

From audio file to text, right on your Mac

Sagt turns recordings into text with timecodes and who said what. Meetings, interviews and lectures become searchable and ready to edit and share. Everything happens on your own Mac. Your audio never leaves the machine.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store · No account · No subscription

How it works

Three steps from recording to finished text

  1. Drop in an audio file

    Drop a recording onto the window: m4a, mp3, wav, aac, flac, ogg or opus, or a video such as mp4, mov, webm or mkv. Several files are queued, with progress and time remaining.

  2. Sagt writes it down

    Whisper runs on your Mac's Neural Engine and GPU. The text appears as it is ready, and Sagt finds the speakers along the way. Name them, and the names appear right in the text.

  3. Search, edit and share

    Click the text to jump in the audio, fix words that came out wrong, and export to Markdown, Word, SRT or plain text, with or without timecodes.

Made for Norwegian, and English

Pick the model that suits the recording and your Mac

For Norwegian, Sagt uses NB-Whisper from the National Library of Norway, a Whisper model trained on large amounts of Norwegian speech. For English, the standard Whisper models do the job.

You can choose between four models, and Sagt recommends the one that suits your Mac. If you are unsure about a result, transcribe the recording again with another model. The old version is kept, and you can compare them in tabs or side by side while the audio plays.

Everything around your recordings

More than a text file

Who said what

Sagt tells the speakers apart while the recording is transcribed. Name them, and the names appear right in the text.

Playback with text

The sentence being played is highlighted. Click a line to jump there in the audio, and play back at 0.75× to 2× speed.

Search everything

Full-text search across all your recordings. Results take you straight to the moment in the audio.

Projects

Group recordings into projects, for example one per client, course or board. Double-click a title to rename it.

Your own words

Teach Sagt names, places and technical terms, so they are spelled right next time.

Export

Markdown, Word, SRT for subtitles, or plain text. With or without timecodes.

Automatic titles

Each recording gets a short, descriptive title, so your library is not full of file names like “Recording 14”.*

Summaries

Get a short summary of the meeting, with decisions and action items listed separately.*

Ask your recordings

Ask a question about one recording, a project or your whole library, and get answers with sources and timestamps.*

* Uses Apple's built-in language model and requires macOS 26 and a Mac with Apple Intelligence. This also runs on your Mac. Transcription works without it.

Privacy

Confidential recordings stay on your Mac

Meetings, interviews and conversations are often confidential. That is why Sagt never uploads audio or text. There is no account, no cloud and no usage tracking. Recordings, transcripts and the search index live in a library folder you choose.

The only network use is downloading models from Hugging Face: the speech model you choose, and a small speaker model (about 25 MB) the first time speakers are detected. After that, Sagt works offline. Read more in the privacy policy.

Common questions

Support for Sagt

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download models. The first time you press “Download”, Sagt fetches the speech model you chose from Hugging Face. The speaker model (about 25 MB) is downloaded the first time speakers are detected. After that, the app works offline.

Which model should I pick?

Start with the one Sagt recommends. For Norwegian speech, NB-Whisper usually gives the best result. On a Mac with little memory, or when you want the text quickly, “Fast” or “Balanced” is a good choice. You can always transcribe again with another model and compare.

Where are my recordings and transcripts stored?

In the library folder you chose. It holds your recordings, transcripts, projects and the search index. Nothing is uploaded.

Why don’t I see titles, summaries or “Ask”?

These features use Apple’s built-in language model and require macOS 26 or later on a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Right after it is turned on, Apple’s model may need some time to get ready. Transcription works as usual without it.

Does anything cost extra?

No. Sagt has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
