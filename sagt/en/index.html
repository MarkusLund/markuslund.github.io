Sagt for Mac

Sagt

Sagt transcribes audio files to Norwegian text, locally on your Mac. Audio never leaves your machine.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

Only once: the first time you press “Download”, Sagt fetches the speech model (Whisper large-v3) from Hugging Face. After that, the app works offline.

Where are my recordings and transcripts stored?

In the library folder you chose. It holds your recordings, transcripts, projects and the search index. Nothing is uploaded.

Why don’t I see titles, summaries or “Ask Project”?

These features use Apple’s built-in language model and require macOS 26 or later on a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Right after it is turned on, Apple’s model may need some time to get ready. Transcription works as usual without it.

Does anything cost extra?

No. Sagt has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

What about privacy?

Sagt collects no data. Read more in the privacy policy.
