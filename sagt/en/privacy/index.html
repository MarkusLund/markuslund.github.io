Sagt for Mac

Privacy Policy

In short: Sagt does not collect any data about you. Audio is transcribed on your own Mac and never leaves it.

No data collection

Sagt has no analytics, tracking, user accounts or advertising, and contains no third-party SDKs for such purposes. The developer receives no data from the app.

Transcription happens on your Mac

Sagt turns audio files into Norwegian text using the Whisper speech recognition model, running on your Mac through WhisperKit and Core ML. Audio is not sent to any server.

Titles, summaries and “Ask Project”

These features use Apple’s built-in language model (Foundation Models, macOS 26 or later), which runs on your device. Your text is not sent to the developer or any other service.

Network connections

The app’s only network connection is the download of the speech model (Whisper large-v3) and its tokenizer files from Hugging Face, the first time you press “Download”. No recordings, transcripts or other app data are sent along. As with any download, Hugging Face may see standard technical information, such as your IP address. Hugging Face has its own privacy policy. After the download, Sagt works offline.

Storage and file access

  • Recordings, transcripts, projects and the search index are stored locally in the library folder you choose. You can delete them at any time.
  • Sagt runs in Apple’s App Sandbox and only reads files you drag into the app or select yourself.

Purchases

Sagt has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
